# Everything you can say

Tap the mic on the phone, speak, then tap **Tap to send**. You can also type the same words in the bar. Prefer
press-and-hold? Switch with the "Mic mode" link at the bottom of the Remote tab.

The phrases below are understood on your PC straight away, without the AI helper. You don't need the exact words:
"hey Armrest, please pause the video" works too. Anything that isn't listed goes to the AI helper (a free Gemini key
or a local model, set up from the Armrest window), which can see what is on the page.

You can chain several: *"mute, new tab and play lofi on YouTube"*.

## Playback

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "pause" · "stop" · "hold on" | Pauses |
| "play" · "resume" · "continue" | Plays |
| "next" · "skip this song" · "previous" | Next or previous video or track |
| "skip ahead 30 seconds" · "forward a minute" · "rewind 20 seconds" · "go back 10 seconds" | Seeks (10 seconds if you don't say) |
| "start over" · "restart the video" · "play from the beginning" | Back to the start |
| "change the speed of the player to 1.25 times speed" · "play at 2x" · "1.5x speed" | Sets the playback speed (0.25x to 4x) |
| "normal speed" · "half speed" · "double speed" · "reset the speed" | Common speeds |
| "speed up" · "faster" · "slow down" · "slower" | Speed in steps of 0.25x |
| "skip the ad" | Skips or closes a video ad |
| "skip intro" · "skip recap" · "skip credits" · "next episode" | Presses the streaming site's own button |

## Volume

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "volume 30" · "set the volume to 30 percent" | Exact volume |
| "volume up" · "louder" · "volume down" · "quieter" | Steps |
| "mute" · "unmute" | Mute on or off |

## Screen

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "fullscreen" · "make it fullscreen" | The player **and** the Edge window go fullscreen, so no tabs show on the TV |
| "exit fullscreen" · "unfullscreen" · "leave full screen" · "minimise the video" | Back to the normal view, tabs included |
| "turn on subtitles" · "subtitles off" · "Spanish subtitles" · "captions in German" | Subtitles in the language you name (English by default) |
| "turn off the screen" | Blanks the TV without stopping anything |

## Finding things to watch

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "play bruno mars on YouTube" · "play lofi on YouTube Music" | Searches and plays the first result |
| "open Netflix" · "open Disney plus" · "go to Twitch" | Opens the site (Netflix, YouTube, YouTube Music, Prime Video, Disney+, Spotify, Twitch, Plex, Crunchyroll, Hulu, Max, Apple TV) |
| "search for bruno mars on YouTube" · "search X on Google" | Shows the results and **waits** for you to choose |
| "search big bang theory" | Types it into the search box of the page you're on |
| "search ariana grande on YouTube Music and play a song featuring the weeknd" | Searches, reads the results and opens the best match (AI helper) |
| "play episode 5" · "open the second result" · "play number 3" | Picks that item on the current page |
| "click on Season 1" · "press Next" · "tap the button that says Subscribe" | Clicks whatever shows those words |
| "what's the capital of Greece?" · "what's the weather today?" | Opens a web search in a new tab |

## Browser

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "new tab" · "close this tab" · "duplicate this tab" | Tabs |
| "next tab" · "previous tab" | Switch tabs |
| "go back" · "go forward" · "reload" | Page history |
| "scroll down" · "scroll up" | Scrolls the page |
| "save this page" · "open my bookmark netflix" · "remove the bookmark for bbc" | Saved pages (also in the phone's **Saved** tab) |
| "turn off pop-up protection" · "block pop-ups" | Lets sites open new tabs, or stops them |

Commands always act on the tab that is showing on the TV, even if you switched tabs with the touchpad or a keyboard.

## Keyboard

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "press F11" · "hit escape" · "press enter" | Presses that key on the PC |
| "press control L" · "press alt left arrow" · "press ctrl and shift tab" | A shortcut |

A few shortcuts are blocked for safety: Ctrl+Alt combinations, Alt+F4, Ctrl+Esc, Ctrl+Shift+Esc and
Ctrl+Shift+Delete. There is no Windows key. Key presses need the touchpad and keys turned on for your phone.

## Timers, pairing and replies

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "set a timer for 10 minutes" | A countdown on the TV |
| "sleep timer 30 minutes" | Pauses the video when the time is up |
| "pair a friend" · "show the QR code" | Shows the pairing code on the TV |
| "hide the QR code" · "reset the QR code" | Hides it, or makes a new one so old codes stop working |
| "stop talking" · "voice replies on" · "use the Aurora voice" · "be brief" | Spoken replies from the TV (also in the phone's Settings) |

## The AI helper

Anything else, such as *"put on the one with the cat"* or *"play the pilot"*, goes to the AI helper. It reads the
items on the current page and picks one. It stays on the site you're watching unless you say *search*, *YouTube* or
*music*. It remembers your last few requests for about 30 minutes, so *"do that again in a new tab"* works. When it
isn't sure, it asks and gives you choices to tap.

The AI helper can only use safe, fixed actions. It cannot press keyboard shortcuts, change settings or open sites
outside the allowed list on its own. Those need your own words.
