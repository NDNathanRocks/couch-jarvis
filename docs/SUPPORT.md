# Armrest support

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will get back to you. To help us help you, attach a diagnostics file: in the Armrest window
on your PC, open Help > Save a diagnostics file for support. It contains no names, addresses, keys or what you watch.

## Quick fixes

- **The phone says the PC is offline.** Is Armrest running on the PC (the icon in the taskbar's tray)? Is the PC awake
  and online? The Armrest window's Status page shows what is wrong, with a button to fix it.
- **Nothing happens on the TV.** Open the Armrest window > Status. If the TV browser is not running, press Open the
  TV browser.
- **Voice commands do nothing.** Allow the microphone for this site in your phone's browser, and check Status > Voice
  on the PC.
- **Free-form commands do not work** but buttons do. The PC needs an AI: add a free Gemini key in Settings, or download
  the local AI (Status > AI brain > Set up AI).
- **A phone should not control the PC any more.** Armrest window > Phones > Remove. Make it a guest instead to give it
  media and volume only.
- **Pairing a new phone.** Click the Armrest icon in the tray to show the QR code on the TV, then scan it.
