# Get Armrest

Your PC, within arm's reach: any phone becomes the remote for the Windows PC on your TV, by voice, buttons, a
touchpad and a keyboard. No accounts and no Wi-Fi setup.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 PC connected to your TV, with Microsoft Edge.
- A phone with a camera and a web browser. Friends' phones work too.

## Microsoft Store (recommended)

[Get Armrest from the Microsoft Store]([STORE LINK]). The Store installs updates for you, and the free trial and
purchase are handled by Microsoft.

## Buy a licence key (website download)

The website version is free to try for 14 days. To keep using it, [buy a licence key]([CHECKOUT LINK]), then paste it
in the Armrest window on your PC under Licence.

## How to start

- Install Armrest on the PC and open it.
- A QR code appears on the TV.
- Scan it with your phone's camera and open the link. You only do this once.
